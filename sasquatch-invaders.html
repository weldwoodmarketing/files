Campsite Invaders

The raccoons raided the campsite! Move Bigfoot and pelt them before they reach the ground. Grab green power-ups to add a projectile each time — hit 5 and they go SUPER. Your ammo evolves every 10 levels: rocks → rockets → poop → raccoon heads → axes (and their wilder powered forms). Each tier adds another boss and a new chapter of the story. Survive all 50 levels to save Squatchkind!

On mobile: drag to move, tap to throw
